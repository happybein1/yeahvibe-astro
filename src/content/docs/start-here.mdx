YeahCore is a set of short, practical guides to help you feel better and decide better. Each one takes a few minutes to read and ends with something you can try today.

Looking for help with something specific right now? [Pick your situation](/) on the home page.

## The story, in 6 chapters

The guides are written to be read in order, like a story. Each one ends with something to try today and a link to the next guide. You can also open any guide on its own.

**Where am I now? → Where do I want to go? → How do I get there? → How do I keep the energy? → How do I make room for it? → How do I do it with others?** Then check in again.
