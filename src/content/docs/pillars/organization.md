> **The Remedy for:** Feeling disorganized, scattered, or physically drained by your environment.

## The Insight

Success is a product of low friction. Most people try to power through a disorganized environment with sheer willpower — and wonder why they're exhausted by noon.

Your surroundings are not neutral. A cluttered desk, a chaotic folder structure, a commute with no purpose — these all drain cognitive resources that could be going toward what actually matters. Optimizing your environment is not about being tidy. It is about designing a context where the right actions become the easiest actions.

When your environment works for you, you stop fighting yourself.

---

## What this pillar covers

1. **The core principles of organization** — systems that maintain themselves with minimal effort.
2. **Digital organization** — a folder structure you can navigate in under 30 seconds.
3. **Reclaiming commuting time** — turning dead time into deliberate learning, planning, or rest.
