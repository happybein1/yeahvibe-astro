YeahVibe is built with the belief that better decision-making tools should be accessible to everyone. That takes a community.

## Why support YeahVibe?

Supporting YeahVibe means helping keep the knowledge base free and openly accessible, enabling the development of new tools, and contributing to a community committed to thoughtful, effective action.

## How you can support

### Share it
Share YeahVibe with someone who would benefit. Word of mouth is the most powerful growth engine we have.

### Give feedback
[One Minute Feedback Form](https://form.jotform.com/222551513307043)

### Join the community
[Join our Discord Server](https://discord.com/invite/62xRJHCbRW)

### Partner with us
If you represent an organization and see alignment with YeahVibe's mission, reach out via Discord or the feedback form.
