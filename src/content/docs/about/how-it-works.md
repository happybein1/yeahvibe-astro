YeahVibe is designed to meet you where you are and guide you toward where you want to be. Here is how the experience works, from your first visit to sustained growth.

---

## Step 1 — Find Your Starting Point

Everyone arrives at YeahVibe with a different challenge. Some feel directionless. Others are overwhelmed, disorganized, or disconnected. Rather than forcing a one-size-fits-all path, YeahVibe helps you identify **your specific lever**.

**Two ways to start:**

- **[Start Here](/start-here/)** — Follow the guided roadmap through the 5 Pillars of Leverage. Read the *Remedy* and *Insight* for each pillar and notice which one resonates most with your current situation.
- **[Self-Assessment Tool](https://form.jotform.com/222552689387066)** — Answer a short set of questions and receive a personalized recommendation for where to focus first.

---

## Step 2 — Read & Absorb

Each article on YeahVibe is written to be:

- **Concise** — no filler, no padding. Every section earns its place.
- **Actionable** — each article ends with something you can apply immediately or this week.
- **Connected** — articles link to related concepts so you can go deeper when ready.

Use the **sidebar** to navigate between topics, or follow the natural sequence within each pillar. The sidebar is organized to mirror the order in which most people benefit from the content.

---

## Step 3 — Apply the Tools

Reading alone is not enough. YeahVibe pairs every insight with practical tools to help you act on what you've learned.

Visit **[Advised Tools](/advised-tools/)** to find:

- **Prioritization templates** — to rank your goals and actions
- **Journaling tools** — to reflect and track your progress
- **AI reflection partners** — to talk through decisions and ideas
- **Self-assessment** — to measure your well-being over time

The goal is a simple cycle: **read → apply → reflect → adapt**.

---

## Step 4 — Listen While You Go

Every article on YeahVibe includes a **Listen** button in the bottom-right corner. Tap it to have the article read aloud in your language — useful when commuting, exercising, or simply when you prefer audio over reading.

The voice automatically matches the language of the page (English or French).

---

## Step 5 — Go Deeper with YeahSight

Once your personal foundations are in place, **[YeahSight](/applications/foresight-trends/)** helps you zoom out and understand the world around you.

YeahSight covers four domains:
- 🌱 **Environment & Energy** — land use, clean energy, diet and its planetary impact
- ⚖️ **Economy & Equity** — economic systems, fair distribution, finance basics
- 🤖 **Technology & Automation** — AI, automation, and the future of work
- 🌐 **Social Organization** — societal systems, redistribution, migration

Understanding these trends helps you make decisions that are not just good for today, but aligned with where the world is heading.

---

## Step 6 — Come Back & Revisit

Growth is not linear. YeahVibe is designed to be revisited — as you evolve, different pillars will become relevant. A challenge that felt distant six months ago may be exactly where you need to focus today.

**Bookmark** your most useful articles. **Share** the ones that resonated with someone who needs them. **Contribute** feedback to help us improve.

---

## The YeahVibe Cycle

> Find your starting point → Read & absorb → Apply the tools → Reflect & adapt → Go deeper (YeahSight) → Revisit as you grow

> **Stop managing time. Start building leverage.**
